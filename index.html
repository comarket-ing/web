Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about co-marketing partnerships

What is co-marketing and how does CoMarket.ing automate it?

Co-marketing is a B2B growth strategy where two or more complementary, non-competing companies collaborate on joint marketing campaigns to share audiences and generate qualified leads together. CoMarket.ing is an AI-powered platform that automates the entire co-marketing workflow: it uses NLP algorithms to generate your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) vector, scans thousands of SaaS companies to find compatible partners (scoring each from 0–100), securely maps accounts to identify audience overlaps, and provides tools to launch co-branded campaigns — all with zero advertising spend.

How does AI partner matching work on CoMarket.ing?

CoMarket.ing's partner matching engine uses Natural Language Processing (NLP) to analyze your product description, key features, and target audience, generating a multi-dimensional ICP vector profile. This vector is compared against thousands of registered SaaS companies using similarity scoring. The system automatically filters out direct competitors (companies with identical core functionality), then scores remaining candidates from 0 to 100 based on audience compatibility, technology stack overlap, hiring signals, recent funding activity, and market positioning. You can also upload a list of your top 10 existing partners for lookalike modeling — the AI analyzes their common traits and finds hundreds of similar companies across other markets and geographies.

How much does CoMarket.ing cost?

CoMarket.ing starts at $20 per month with unlimited partner discovery. Pricing is volume-based — you only pay for what you use, with no inflated costs for growing partner networks or inactive connections. For context, traditional B2B lead acquisition through Google Ads costs $45–$150 per lead, while LinkedIn Ads range from $75–$200 per lead. A single successful partnership through CoMarket.ing can generate 200+ qualified leads, delivering approximately $9,000 in equivalent lead acquisition value for $20/month.

Is my CRM data safe when using account mapping?

Yes. CoMarket.ing uses a privacy-first architecture with OAuth 2.0 authentication for all CRM connections (Salesforce, HubSpot, and others). You have granular control over what data is shared with each partner — ranging from fully anonymous overlap counts to detailed company profiles. Your CRM data never leaves your secure environment until you explicitly authorize sharing. The platform also supports custom population segmentation (Prospects, Open Opportunities, Customers) and inclusion/exclusion filters, so you control exactly which records participate in account mapping.

What CRM integrations does CoMarket.ing support?

CoMarket.ing integrates with Salesforce, HubSpot, and other major CRM platforms via OAuth 2.0. The platform supports real-time webhook-based synchronization with Fibonacci-backoff retry logic for reliability, custom population segmentation, granular inclusion filters, and bi-directional sync for deal stage updates. You can also upload CSV files for manual data import. Typical CRM integration takes under 30 minutes.

How does CoMarket.ing compare to Crossbeam, Reveal, or PartnerStack?

CoMarket.ing differentiates in three key ways: (1) AI-powered partner discovery — while Crossbeam and Reveal require you to already know your partners, CoMarket.ing algorithmically finds ideal partners you haven't met yet using ICP vector matching; (2) Pricing — CoMarket.ing starts at $20/month vs enterprise-only pricing at Crossbeam and Reveal; (3) Focus — PartnerStack manages affiliate and referral programs, while CoMarket.ing is purpose-built for co-marketing partnerships including co-branded campaigns, joint content, and audience exchange. CoMarket.ing is the only platform that combines partner discovery, secure account mapping, and campaign management in one tool at a price point accessible to startups and SMBs.

Core Capabilities

AI Partner Discovery
NLP-based ICP vector profiling that scans thousands of SaaS companies, filters competitors, and scores matches from 0–100 with a detailed signal breakdown including hiring activity, funding rounds, technology stack, and market overlap.

Overlap Analytics
Real-time dashboards showing overlap percentages, lead quality scores, and conversion rates across all active partnerships. Visual overlap matrix comparing populations (Prospects × Partner Customers).
